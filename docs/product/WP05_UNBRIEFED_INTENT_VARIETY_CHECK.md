# WP05 Unbriefed Intent and Variety Check

Status: **fixed procedure; pending selected production implementation and genuine owner-run participants**

This procedure cannot be executed against Part A development prototypes as a WP05 acceptance pass. It exists now so production tuning cannot rewrite the test after seeing participant outcomes.

## Cohort and build record

- Owner designates five participants not involved in implementation before observation.
- Do not replace a failed scored participant with a later participant.
- Record build/content revision, schema, seed, crew/build, device/input, run outcome, and eligible timing.
- Use representative fixed-seed sessions spanning Jax, Zoey, and Rex plus the three WP04 builds.

## Prompt

Give only:

> Play this district run. Tell me what you think each enemy is about to do, when you choose to intervene, and when you deliberately hold an available action.

Do not explain Environment, Focus, Backup, target priority, counters, intended strong cases, or desired conclusions.

## Required observations

For each participant record without coaching:

1. Whether they identify at least one Bat, Bottle, Enforcer, or Viper intent before resolution.
2. The first Environment, Focus, and Backup opportunity they notice.
3. One accepted use and predicted primary result before activation.
4. One deliberate hold/alternative and the stated reason.
5. Any colour-only, clipped, obscured, late, ambiguous, or precision-input cue.
6. Any action used automatically whenever available and why.
7. Three meaningful decisions they recall after the session.

## Pass threshold

- At least 4/5 identify current target/intent and one valid response before the decision window closes.
- At least 4/5 explain one strong intervention use and one reason to hold or choose another role.
- At least 4/5 name three distinct meaningful decisions across plan/build/intervention/push play.
- No permanent action is broadly treated as universally correct whenever available.
- Important validity, target, timing, charge/cooldown, activation, rejection, expiry, and result information is understood without relying on colour alone.

Record every failure and exact wording. Automated probes, implementers, screenshots, and coached demonstrations cannot satisfy this gate. Only the owner may enter the final result.
