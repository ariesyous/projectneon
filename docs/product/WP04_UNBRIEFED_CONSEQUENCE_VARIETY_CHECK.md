# WP04 Unbriefed Consequence and Variety Check

Status: **PENDING OWNER EXECUTION — no participant evidence recorded**

Only the owner or designated observer may designate the cohort, record observations, and make the qualitative decision. Coding agents, implementers, screenshots, and automation cannot fill the record.

## Fixed procedure

1. Record candidate build/content revision, platform/input, seed/schema, and a fixed five-person cohort before observation. Do not replace a failed scored participant.
2. Give only: **“Choose your gear or shop action. Tell me what you expect to change before you confirm, then point out when you see that change in the next fight.”**
3. Do not explain equipment tags, synergy thresholds, Heat tiers, the intended build identities, or desired answer.
4. Across the cohort, cover Jax/Knockback, Zoey/Tech, and Rex/Bleed fixed builds from the audit plus one full-inventory and one shop visit per participant.
5. Before Confirm/Skip/Leave, record whether the participant independently names the selected item/action, exact destination/outgoing item, active/backpack state, synergy gain/loss, important before/after value, affordability/stock effect, and primary next-fight consequence.
6. During the next fight, record whether the participant recognizes one build expression (status proc, faster cadence/intervention, environmental hit, or survival effect) without coaching.
7. Ask why one offered alternative was rejected and whether they would choose differently for another crew/threat.
8. Record failures verbatim and do not reinterpret partial answers into a pass.

## Scored criteria

A participant passes only if they:

- predict the representative equipment/shop choice's primary consequence before confirmation;
- identify its next applicable expression;
- understand active versus backpack and the named outgoing/discard consequence;
- understand Skip Gear keeps the paired reward;
- state affordability and finite stock correctly for the sampled shop;
- distinguish at least three build intentions across the cohort without treating one starter/build as universally best.

WP04 passes at **4/5 or better** for consequence and variety, consistent with `GameSpecifications.md` section 0.6.
