# WP02 Unbriefed Comprehension Check

Status: **Prepared; not yet executed.** Only the project owner or designated observer records results.

## Fixed procedure

1. Record build/content revision, platform, input, seed/schema, and the five-person scored cohort before the first observation. Additional participants cannot replace a failed scored observation.
2. Give no feature or system briefing. Say only: **“Play until you reach a run decision. Tell me what phase you are in, what happens next, and what you can do now whenever I ask.”**
3. Do not define PLAN, block, reward, Heat, Night Pressure, Extract, Push, final commitment, or the expected sequence. Do not correct an answer during the scored session.
4. At one PLAN, FIGHT/arrival, REWARD or SHOP, and LAP DECISION state, ask the same three questions: current phase, next event, available action.
5. Before the participant confirms a lap decision, ask what Extract does, what Push does, and what state they expect immediately after confirmation. At the second decision, ask whether routine extraction will reopen during lap 3 and what ends that lap.
6. After at least one completed lap, ask the participant to recount the repeating run sequence in their own words.
7. Record concise, unattributed answers and observable confusion. Record failures; do not reinterpret partial answers into a pass.

## Scored criteria

A participant passes only if all of these are independently correct:

- identifies current phase, named next event, and available action in the sampled states;
- distinguishes tactical/coolable Heat from irreversible Night Pressure;
- explains that Extract secures the current result and ends the run;
- explains that Push after lap 1 enters lap 2 with higher risk/reward;
- explains that Push after lap 2 commits to final lap 3 and The Viper, with no routine lap-3 extraction;
- recounts `PLAN -> BLOCK/FIGHT (or SHOP/UTILITY) -> REWARD ->` repeat three times `-> LAP DECISION`, then the boss after the final lap;
- predicts the immediate post-confirm state correctly.

WP02 passes at **4/5 or better**. Automation, screenshots, observer inference, or a coding agent cannot fill these rows.
