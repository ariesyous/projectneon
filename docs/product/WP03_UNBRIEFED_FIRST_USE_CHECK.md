# WP03 Unbriefed First-Use Check

Status: **PENDING OWNER EXECUTION — no observations or pass recorded**

This is the qualitative remainder of the WP03 gate. Only the project owner may designate the scored cohort, record observations, and make the acceptance decision. Automated tests, screenshots, implementers, and coding agents are not participants and cannot fill this record.

## Fixed procedure

1. Use the WP03 candidate build and a fresh production-profile state.
2. Designate five participants who were not briefed on the implementation or desired answer. Do not replace a failed scored observation with an extra participant.
3. Give only: **“Choose what should happen next. Tell me what you expect before you confirm, then tell me when you see it happen.”**
4. Do not explain cards, deck state, Heat, the expected block, the intended UI hierarchy, or the acceptance threshold before the observation.
5. For each participant, record whether they:
   - selected and confirmed a valid first card without intervention;
   - predicted the primary next-block type/effect before confirmation;
   - noticed and identified the matching expression when the next block occurred;
   - mistook the choice for a persistent combat hand, future-slot planner, or optional dismissible layer;
   - needed facilitator help, and exactly what help was given.
6. Record concise unattributed notes and the candidate build/seed. Do not infer a pass from automated evidence.
